Zichen Liu

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Xusheng Xiao.

Previously, I obtained my M.S. in ECE from the University of Washington and my B.S. in Physics from Lanzhou University (Cuiying Honors College).

My research interests lie at the intersection of Software Engineering, Program Analysis, and Machine Learning. Currently, I focus on leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance mobile app privacy analysis and behavior description.
